Your instructor

Dr. Yu Sun

Professor of Computer Science, Cal Poly Pomona

Dr. Sun brings industry experience from Amazon and several tech startups into the classroom. He teaches the way products are really built: start with an idea, ship it, and improve it with real users.

At Cal Poly Pomona he is a founding faculty member of the iLab, the university's innovation and entrepreneurship lab, where he mentors student founders.

Questions students ask

Before you enroll.

I have never coded. Can I take this?

Yes. The course is designed for students with no coding background.

I'm a CS major. Will I learn anything new?

Yes. You'll learn to direct AI across a whole product and ship far more than you could by hand.

What do I need?

A laptop and an idea you are curious about.

Does it count toward my degree?

It depends on your major and catalog year. Check with your academic advisor.
